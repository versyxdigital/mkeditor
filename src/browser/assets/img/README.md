# Google brand assets

The following official assets are bundled unchanged for offline use. Google Drive and Google are trademarks of Google LLC.

- `google-drive.png`: [Google Drive product logo](https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/productlogos/drive_2026/v2/web-64dp/logo_drive_2026_color_2x_web_64dp.png), linked by Google's [Drive branding guidance](https://developers.google.com/workspace/drive/api/guides/branding).
- `google.png`: [Google sign-in icon](https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png), linked by Google's [sign-in branding guidance](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines).

Display these at their original aspect ratio, retaining the official colors. Do not recolor them to match the application theme. The sign-in buttons use a white background for the Google icon; other app controls retain MKEditor's shared palette.
